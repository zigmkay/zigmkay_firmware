# AI usage

Parts of ZigMkay were developed with AI assistance.

All AI-generated code, features and changes were reviewed and tested by human
developers before inclusion.

The same rule applies to new contributions: AI-assisted changes are reviewed
and tested by a human before they are merged, like any other change. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Questions about this policy are welcome on the
[ZigMkay Discord](https://discord.gg/bJCZUtJD65) or in a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/zigmkay/zigmkay_firmware/issues).
